03 · Security

Trust boundaries and forbidden flows

The design assumes every monitored source may contain instructions aimed at the agent. Safety comes from fixed capabilities, typed transitions, immutable evidence, and explicit “no path” controls—not from hoping a model ignores the payload.

STRIDE-reviewed External content = data Unknown fields rejected No source-derived actions

04 · Local AI

Two rails: authority and observation

The deterministic rail publishes. The local-inference rail measures semantic recall and writes telemetry back into the report. The rails touch at input and metrics—never at a decision edge.

05 · Data

Who owns what

Four durable layers have deliberately different jobs. The portal and orchestration surfaces are consumers; neither is allowed to become the system of record.

06 · Topology

What runs where

The production profile is intentionally local-first. Internet egress is source-specific; storage and inference bind to loopback; only the read-only portal is available on the trusted private LAN.
