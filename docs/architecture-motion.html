01 · Observe

Many surfaces, separate identities

Social, web, GitHub, and authoritative records stay attributable. Reposts do not become fake corroboration.

02 · Contain

Assume the payload is hostile

Destination, DNS, redirects, MIME, bytes, archives, malware, and prompt injection are checked before model access.

03 · Prove

Turn content into evidence

Immutable hashes, sanitized excerpts, typed observations, source lineage, and deterministic scores form the case.

04 · Challenge

Fan out, then reconverge

Specialist, impact, and skeptic review in parallel. Citation verification runs before bounded coordination.

05 · Decide

Keep authority in code

Claims, citations, confidence, and publication class pass deterministic checks. Models cannot press publish.

06 · Improve

Measure without self-editing

Acceptance, agent, source, and replay evals can emit a proposal. Human approval and rollback stay mandatory.
